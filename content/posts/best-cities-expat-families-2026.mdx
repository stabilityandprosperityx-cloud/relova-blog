**best cities expat families 2026** Ranking family cities means weighting schools, pediatric ER wait times, air quality, and parental sanity—not just tax rates. Singapore still leads on safety and STEM pipelines but international school invoices hit $20,000–40,000 yearly. Amsterdam packs 240+ nationalities into progressive public tracks with cycling infrastructure unmatched. Vienna repeatedly tops liveability indexes with nearly free university pathways later. Lisbon combines Mediterranean safety narratives with international schools €8,000–18,000. Dubai offers tax-free packages and British curricula at AED 30,000–80,000. Kuala Lumpur undercuts everyone on tuition at MYR 25,000–60,000. Auckland, Prague, Porto, and Berlin round out the shortlist for balance.
