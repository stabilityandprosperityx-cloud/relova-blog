**Canggu vs Ubud vs Seminyak Bali 2026** is three different islands in one island. **Canggu** has Asia’s densest nomad coworking stack (Dojo Bali, Canggu Coworking) and the worst peak traffic on Jl. Batu Bolong and Jl. Pantai Berawa (**16:00–19:00**). **Ubud** is quieter, cheaper, and better for deep work—with weaker internet. **Seminyak** is the polished restaurant/nightlife strip, least oriented to remote work. ## Neighborhood profiles and 2026 rents

One-bed villa rents: Canggu **$800–1,500** (Pererenan/Berawa usually under Batu Bolong), Ubud **$400–900**, Seminyak **$1,200–2,500**. Comfortable monthly budgets: Canggu **$1,200–2,200**, Ubud **$1,000–1,600**, Seminyak **$1,500–3,000**. Internet averages about **50–100 Mbps** in Canggu, **20–60** in Ubud, and often unstable in Seminyak beach clubs. Canggu rents rose roughly **30–50%** since 2020—the “cheap paradise” label no longer fits that strip.

## Visas: E33G, Second Home, and B211A

Indonesia’s **E33G Remote Worker Visa** (from April 2024) needs about **$60,000/year** income, lasts **one year**, and is not multi-extendable from inside—you exit and reapply. **Second Home** offers **5–10 years** with roughly **$130,000–133,000** on deposit. **B211A** (60 days, extendable toward 180) remains the workhorse for many, but remote-work legality is a grey zone—not an explicit remote-work permit.

## Tax residency after 183 days

Spend **183 days** in Indonesia within 12 months and you can become a tax resident on **worldwide** income with progressive rates up to **35%**. Structure remittances and entity choices before you cross that line.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Which Bali area is best for digital nomads?**

**Canggu** for community and coworking; **Ubud** for focus and lower cost; **Seminyak** if nightlife matters more than wifi.

**How expensive is Canggu now?**

Comfortable budgets **$1,200–2,200**; one-bed villas **$800–1,500**. Rents rose about **30–50%** since 2020.

**What visa should remote workers use?**

**E33G** if you clear ~**$60,000/year**; otherwise many still use **B211A** in a legal grey zone. Wealthier applicants look at **Second Home** (~**$130k** deposit).

**When do I become an Indonesian tax resident?**

After about **183 days** in 12 months—then worldwide income can face rates up to **35%**.

**Is Ubud’s internet good enough?**

Often **20–60 Mbps**—fine for many workflows, weaker than Canggu’s **50–100 Mbps** for heavy video.
