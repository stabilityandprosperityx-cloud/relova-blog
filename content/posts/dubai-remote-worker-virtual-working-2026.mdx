From **27 January 2026**, applicants must submit **six months** of bank statements (previously three)—so you generally need half a year of documented income on the current arrangement before filing. Incomplete financial packs are now **automatically refused**; officers no longer treat missing pages as a casual top-up request.

## The 27 January 2026 document crackdown

Bank statements: **6 months** required from **27 January 2026** (was 3). Build the trail before you book medicals. Incomplete financial documentation now triggers **automatic rejection** under early-2026 practice. ## Income floors, fees, and processing speed

Employees commonly need **$3,500/month** (the figure most often cited from GDRFA briefings). Business owners typically need **$5,000/month** plus proof of ownership for at least **one year**—some consultants quote $5,000 for employees too; verify the live GDRFA checklist the week you apply. The visa lasts **1 year** and renews only via a **fresh application**, not auto-renewal. All-in Dubai cost is about **AED 1,225** (~**$334**), including GDRFA base fee (**AED 200**), medical, and Emirates ID. Clean files often clear in **48 hours to 5–7 working days**.

## Tax, absences, family, and long-term limits

UAE personal income tax is **0%**, but your home-country or tax-residence rules still apply. Staying **outside the UAE more than six consecutive months** can cancel the visa. Qualifying income and insurance can let you sponsor a spouse and children for the same term. This route does **not** lead directly to permanent residence or citizenship—longer horizons usually need a separate path such as [UAE Golden Visa](https://blog.relova.ai/blog/uae-golden-visa-guide) via property or business.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What changed on 27 January 2026?**

Bank statements must cover **six months** instead of three, and incomplete financial packages are **automatically rejected**.

**How much income do I need?**

Most employee filings cite **$3,500/month**; business owners typically **$5,000/month** with at least one year of ownership evidence—confirm GDRFA’s current PDF.

**How long does the visa last, and what does it cost?**

**One year**, renewed only by reapplying. Budget about **AED 1,225** (~**$334**) in Dubai for fees, medical, and Emirates ID.

**How fast is processing?**

Often **48 hours to 5–7 working days** when the file is complete.

**Does Virtual Working lead to PR?**

No. For longer stays, plan a separate route such as Golden Visa via property or business. Also avoid **6+ months** continuous absence or the visa can be cancelled.
