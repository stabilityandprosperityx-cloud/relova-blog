## The temporary-housing first method

Arrive on Airbnb/short-stay for **2–4 weeks**. Use that window to see flats in daylight, meet agents, and compare districts. Photos are often outdated, cropped, or stolen. Always insist on a **written contract**—even for a shared room—and pay only via **traceable bank transfer**, never unreceipted cash.

## Fraud red flags that never change

Walk away if anyone demands **wire, crypto, or gift cards** before a viewing. “I’m abroad—I’ll mail the keys after you pay” is **always** a scam. Luxury listing far below market rent is another tell. Pre-recorded video only (no live tour) is a warning. Template emails, spelling chaos, and fake urgency (“another tenant is ready to pay”) complete the pattern. Run **reverse image search**—stolen MLS/agency photos show up constantly.

## Country-specific traps and safer platforms

**South Korea’s jeonse** can mean a deposit up to **100%** of the property value instead of monthly rent—attractive, but fraud losses nationally have exceeded **$1 billion/year**. In **Germany**, some landlords illegally demand extra payment after deposit and signature—that is a violation. In **Japan**, **reikin** (key money) of **1–2 months** is non-refundable; some agencies avoid it. In **Spain** and much of Europe, deposits are often capped near **one month**; Spanish primary-residence agency fees (from **2023** rules) typically fall on the landlord—not for short/seasonal lets. Platforms with renter protections include **HousingAnywhere** (48-hour post-move-in protection) and **Flatio** (medium-term rentals without deposit). Budget beyond rent: deposit **1–3 months** plus agent fee often ~**1 month**.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Should I sign a lease from abroad?**

No. Use **2–4 weeks** temporary housing first, then view live (or on a real-time video call) before any deposit.

**What is the clearest rental scam signal?**

Payment before viewing—especially wire, crypto, or gift cards—or “I’ll mail the keys from overseas.”

**How do I check if photos are stolen?**

**Reverse image search.** Agency watermarks and duplicate listings elsewhere are common tells.

**What country-specific costs surprise people?**

Korea’s **jeonse** mega-deposits, Japan’s **reikin**, and illegal post-signing demands in Germany. Spain often limits deposits near one month for primary homes.

**How much cash beyond the first month’s rent?**

Plan deposit **1–3 months** plus an agent fee near **one month**, on top of rent.
