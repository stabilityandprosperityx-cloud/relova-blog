## Rent, budget, and the real monthly numbers

Plan for Las Palmas 1BR at roughly EUR 650-1,000 (full-city amenities). Maspalomas 1BR comes in at EUR 600-950 (south resort lifestyle). Expect Puerto de Mogan 1BR at EUR 550-800 (quiet premium village). Budget 7% for IGIC (structural cost advantage).

Nomad budget is typically EUR 1,600-2,400/mo (realistic planning range).
