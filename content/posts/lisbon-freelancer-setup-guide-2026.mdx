## NIF, Inicio de Atividade, and recibos verdes

Get a **NIF** first—banks, leases, and activity registration all need it (remote via a representative is possible). File **Início de Atividade** online on the Portal das Finanças or in person: pick a regime (simplified up to **€200,000/year** turnover, or organized accounting) and an activity code (**CAE/CIRS**). Issue a **recibo verde** for every payment through the portal, typically within **five days**. Invoices to foreign (non-Portuguese) tax numbers **cannot be cancelled** on the portal—only a replacement document plus a notice via **e-balcão**.

## VAT from 2026, withholding, and social security

Article 53 VAT exemption applies up to **€15,000/year**. From **1 January 2026** that threshold is a hard edge: charge VAT from the **next month** after you cross it—not from the following calendar year. Portuguese clients may withhold **23–25%**; foreign clients usually do not—you declare in the annual return. Social contributions are **21.4%** of the taxable base, with a **12-month** start-up exemption.

## Simplified tax math and the NHR/IFICI shift

Under the simplified regime, only **75%** of income is taxable (25% is a presumed expense allowance). Personal rates are progressive **14.5–48%**. Do not plan a new **NHR** filing; existing holders keep their 10-year window. **IFICI** is the limited successor for narrow innovation categories—confirm eligibility before you move for tax reasons alone.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the first step for freelancing in Lisbon?**

Obtain a **NIF**, then file **Início de Atividade** before you invoice clients.

**What changed for VAT in 2026?**

Crossing **€15,000/year** now triggers VAT from the **next month**, not from the following year.

**Do I pay social security immediately?**

Usually not—the first **12 months** are often contribution-free, then **21.4%** of the base.

**Can I still get NHR?**

Not as a new applicant after **31 December 2023**. Look at **IFICI** only if you fit its narrow categories.

**Can I cancel a recibo verde issued to a foreign client?**

Not through the normal portal cancel flow—issue a replacement and notify via **e-balcão**.
