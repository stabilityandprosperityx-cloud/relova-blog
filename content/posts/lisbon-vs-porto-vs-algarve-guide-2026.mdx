## Same visas, different real budgets

National thresholds do not change by region. What changes is rent and seasonality. Lisbon center one-beds often **€1,200–1,800**; Porto **€700–1,200** (about **25–35%** cheaper overall); Algarve off-peak **€700–1,200** in Lagos/Faro/Albufeira, with summer rents up **20–40%**.

## Lisbon, Porto, and Algarve profiles

**Lisbon** is the career and international-school hub with the largest expat stack—still cheaper than many EU capitals, but the priciest of the three. **Porto** wins price/atmosphere balance; the same D7 floor buys more comfort here. **Algarve** is beach-and-retiree territory with British/Nordic communities and sharp seasonal pricing. Choose Lisbon for career infrastructure, Porto for value, Algarve for sun-and-sand retirement rhythm.

## Schools and climate

Most international-school choice sits in Lisbon’s western belt (Cascais–Estoril); Porto and Algarve have fewer, cheaper options. Climate: Lisbon and Porto are Atlantic (Porto cooler and wetter); Algarve is Portugal’s driest, sunniest stretch.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Which region is cheapest?**

Usually **Porto**—about **25–35%** under Lisbon. Algarve can match Porto off-peak, then jumps **20–40%** in summer.

**Do visa income floors differ by city?**

No. **D7 ~€920** and **D8 ~€3,680** are national—but livability on those numbers differs sharply.

**Where should families look for schools?**

Lisbon’s Cascais–Estoril belt has the widest international choice; Porto and Algarve are thinner but cheaper.

**Who should pick the Algarve?**

Beach-and-retirement lifestyles—with budget for summer rent spikes.

**Is Lisbon still “cheap Europe”?**

Relative to many capitals, yes—but it is the most expensive of these three Portuguese options.
