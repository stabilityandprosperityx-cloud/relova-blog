## 365-day U.S. entry and residence paperwork

Americans can stay **365 days** without a visa. Beyond that year, residence runs through **e-Albania**—a portal that frequently crashes in practice, so many expats hire a lawyer. Realistic legal/admin spend for a residence file is about **$1,700–2,150** all-in (translations, filing, advice)—separate from monthly living costs and from one-time furniture/setup spend.

## Saranda, Ksamil, and rising Vlora

Sea-view rents in **Saranda** often **€300–500/month** in low season; **July–August** can roughly **double**. **Ksamil** follows the same seasonal spike. **Vlora** is the rising coast story: the new airport moved the property market up while prices are still often below Saranda/Ksamil peaks.

## Budgets, self-employment, and healthcare

Comfortable solo living is commonly **€800–1,200/month**; couples **€1,200–1,800**. Self-employed residents must budget **quarterly** social and health contributions—different from a non-business resident’s monthly burn. Most expats use private clinics for routine care; cash visits are typically affordable. Keep setup costs (furniture, kitchen kit) off your monthly spreadsheet so you do not underfund the first quarter.
