**move to Berlin digital nomad 2026** does **not** use a dedicated digital nomad visa. The practical path is the **Freiberufler** self-employment permit under **Section 21 AufenthG**: first an **Einreise-Visum** (~3 months) from your home consulate, then a full Freiberufler residence permit at the local **Ausländerbehörde**. ## Freiberufler vs Gewerbe, and the two-step process

**Freiberufler** roles (writers, designers, developers, consultants, translators) usually clear more easily than **Gewerbetreibende** trades that need a **Gewerbeschein**. Initial permission often runs **6 months to 3 years**, renewable if the activity continues. Expect a lease of at least **six months** in your filing pack.

## Money, insurance, and age-45+ rules

There is no fixed income floor, but you must show financial viability. Applicants **45+** typically need pension savings equivalent to about **€1,332/month** by retirement age, or assets from about **€195,000**. Health insurance is mandatory for freelancers—no opt-out. Tax: roughly **€9,700/year** personal allowance, then progressive **14–45%**.

## Berlin rents, Anmeldung, and first banking

Center one-beds (Mitte, Prenzlauer Berg, Kreuzberg, Friedrichshain) often **€1,000–1,500**; WG rooms **€500–800**. Comfortable solo budgets **€2,200–3,000** all-in. Book **Anmeldung** via service.berlin.de early—slots vanish fast. **N26** or **Revolut** can open on passport alone before Anmeldung, useful for the first weeks.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does Germany have a digital nomad visa?**

No. Berlin remote workers usually use the **Freiberufler** self-employment route.

**What is the two-step process?**

Consular **Einreise-Visum** first, then the full residence permit at the **Ausländerbehörde** after arrival.

**Do I need a long lease to apply?**

Yes—plan at least **six months** of housing evidence in the file.

**What extra rules hit applicants over 45?**

Show pension provisioning near **€1,332/month** at retirement age, or roughly **€195,000** in assets.

**How do I handle banking before Anmeldung?**

Open **N26/Revolut** on passport, then complete **Anmeldung** as soon as slots appear.
