## How the 2026 SMMLV hike reset visa floors

**SMMLV = COP 1,750,905/month** after the ~**23%** increase. Multiply carefully: **3×** ≈ **COP 5,252,715/month**; **10×** ≈ **COP 17,509,050/month**. Visas are typically issued/renewed in **two-year** blocks without a hard renewal cap. Resident visa (**R**) often follows **2–5 years** on an M-category route depending on subcategory. ## Nomad vs Pensionado vs Rentista

- **Digital Nomad (V Nómadas Digitales):** **3× SMMLV** ≈ **COP 5,252,715/month** (~**$1,375–1,400**) from remote foreign work.  
- **Pensionado (M-11):** same **3×** cash floor (~**$1,380–1,410**), but only **lifetime pension** income (social security / public or private pension)—**not** rent or dividends.  
- **Rentista:** **10× SMMLV** ≈ **COP 17,509,050/month** (~**$4,585**) for passive streams such as rentals and dividends. Mixing these labels is the classic Bogotá application error.

## Bogotá budgets, housing, and timelines

Comfortable solo living often runs **$2,200–3,200/month** in 2026; lean budgets **$1,200–1,700**. Good-area one-beds (e.g. **Chicó**) commonly **$800–1,600/month** (**COP 3–5 million**). After fee payment, visa decisions often land within **~30 days**; full process including **cédula** usually **4–10 weeks**. For another LatAm cost stack, see [Costa Rica](https://blog.relova.ai/blog/move-to-costa-rica-guide-2026).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why did Colombia visa numbers jump in 2026?**

The minimum wage (**SMMLV**) rose ~**23%** to **COP 1,750,905** on **1 January 2026**, and SMMLV-linked visa multiples scaled with it.

**Is Pensionado the same threshold as Rentista?**

No. Both nomad and Pensionado sit near **3× SMMLV** (~**$1,400**), but Rentista needs **10×** (~**$4,585**). Pensionado also requires true pension income.

**How long are visas valid?**

Typically renewable in **two-year** periods without a fixed renewal limit. Resident (**R**) status often after **2–5 years** on M routes.

**What should I budget in Bogotá?**

Comfortable solo: **$2,200–3,200/month**. Lean: **$1,200–1,700**. Chicó-style one-beds: **$800–1,600**.

**How fast is processing?**

Often up to **~30 days** after paying fees; with cédula, plan **4–10 weeks** end-to-end.
