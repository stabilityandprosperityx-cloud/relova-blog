Brisbane isn't the visa story most people arrive expecting — there's no Brisbane-specific permit, and Queensland state nomination just adds five points to whichever federal skilled visa you're already chasing. The **Subclass 189** (Skilled Independent, no sponsor or state nomination needed) has a **65-point floor**, but that's an entry ticket to the invitation queue, not a guarantee: in-demand occupations like software engineering and accounting are currently being invited at **85–95 points** in practice, while less contested ANZSCO codes clear closer to the minimum. **Subclass 190** (state-nominated) adds 5 nomination points to the same 65-point base and is where Queensland's own occupation list — refreshed periodically and narrower than the national one — becomes the deciding factor. **Subclass 491** (Skilled Regional) adds 15 nomination points and requires settling outside Australia's five largest cities for the qualifying period; Brisbane, notably, does **not** count as regional for 491 purposes, so anyone chasing that route is looking at the Sunshine Coast or further out, not the CBD.

## What "cheaper than Sydney" actually costs

The reputation is accurate but the gap has narrowed. A one-bedroom in central Brisbane now runs roughly **AUD 2,200–3,400 a month**, with outer suburbs down around **AUD 1,400–2,500** — Numbeo's September 2026 snapshot puts the citywide average net monthly wage at about **AUD 5,875**, which is the number to weigh those rents against rather than a national or Sydney-anchored figure. Tech and engineering roles typically clear **AUD 95,000–120,000** a year, and that range has held roughly steady through the run-up to the 2032 Olympics even as construction-sector wages have climbed faster on the back of the infrastructure pipeline (Cross River Rail, the Gabba redevelopment, and the broader Brisbane 2032 build schedule). Budget for a car or the growing but still-patchy rail network depending on which suburb you land in — greenfield outer suburbs are cheaper on rent and noticeably more expensive on transport time.

## The Olympics effect is a housing effect, not a jobs flood

The common assumption is that a home Olympics means a hiring surge across the board; the more accurate read is that it's concentrated in construction, hospitality, event logistics, and transport infrastructure roles through the back half of the decade, while broader tech and professional hiring tracks the same national skilled-occupation lists everyone else in Australia competes against. What it does reliably move is housing supply and price pressure — expect rental competition to tighten further as 2032 approaches and construction crews, then event staff, add to demand in a market that was already running tight before the Games were awarded.

## Climate and the subtropical trade-off

Brisbane logs **300-plus sunshine days a year**, a genuine quality-of-life draw against [Melbourne](/blog/move-to-sydney-melbourne-guide-2026)'s changeable weather and Sydney's cost — but it comes with a humid subtropical summer, a real cyclone-season watch (even if direct hits are rare this far south), and home insurance premiums that have risen faster than the national average on flood-zone reassessments since the 2022 flooding. Check a property's flood-overlay status before signing a lease, not after.

## Superannuation and the paperwork that follows you home

One detail skilled migrants routinely miss: Australia's compulsory **superannuation** contributions (currently 12% of ordinary earnings, paid by the employer on top of salary) build a retirement balance that isn't simply cash in a bank account — accessing or transferring it if you later leave Australia involves its own set of rules (the Departing Australia Superannuation Payment, or DASP, for those on temporary visas who permanently depart) that catch people off guard years after they've stopped thinking about immigration paperwork. Worth understanding at the point you accept a job offer, not the point you're leaving.

Brisbane in 2026 is a **65-points-minimum, AUD 2,200–3,400-for-a-one-bed, 2032-shaped** market. The visa math is federal, not municipal — get your ANZSCO occupation and points score sorted before you fall for the sunshine.
