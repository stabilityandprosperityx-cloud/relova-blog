## The €150,000 property-residency rule

Temporary residence linked to property is issued for **one year** and renewed annually while you own a qualifying asset. The hard filter is the **taxable assessed value ≥ €150,000**. A charming **€50,000** two-bedroom fixer can still be a lifestyle buy—it just **will not** unlock the residence track under the new law. Confirm assessment with the Tax Administration before you wire deposits. Country overview: [Move to Montenegro](https://blog.relova.ai/blog/move-to-montenegro-guide-2026).

## Company route, PR, and citizenship

Alternative path: register a local **LLC** (share capital from about **€1**) and show real activity—not a shelf company. Permanent residence typically follows **five years** of continuous temporary residence; citizenship another **five years** after PR (**~10 years** total). Naturalized citizens usually must **renounce** prior citizenship—Montenegro does not broadly allow dual citizenship for naturalization (exceptions exist). ## Kotor living costs and market prices

Old Town **1–2 bedroom** rents often land near **€800/month**. A comfortable couple budget is **€1,500–2,000/month**. National new-build averages in **Q4 2025** were about **€2,206/m²**, rising to roughly **€2,570/m²** on the coast—useful when you reverse-engineer whether a unit can clear the **€150,000** tax value.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I still get Montenegro residence by buying any apartment?**

No. From **17 January 2026** the Tax Administration must assess the property at **€150,000+**. Listing price alone is not enough.

**What if I already had residence via a cheaper property?**

Holders who obtained residence **before 17 January 2026** are covered by transitional rules and can typically renew without meeting the new floor.

**Is there another path besides property?**

Yes—operate a real Montenegrin **LLC** (capital can be as low as **€1**) with documented activity.

**How long to PR and a passport?**

About **5 years** temporary residence to PR, then roughly **5 more** to citizenship. Expect to renounce most other citizenships on naturalization.

**What does life in Kotor cost?**

Old Town 1–2BR rent ~**€800/month**; comfortable couple spend **€1,500–2,000/month**. Coast new-builds averaged ~**€2,570/m²** in late 2025.
