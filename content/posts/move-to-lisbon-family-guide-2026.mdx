## Law 61/2025 and family filing strategy

Under **Law 61/2025**, staggered family reunification got harder for adult dependents. The practical fix is simultaneous filing so spouses and children arrive on the same timeline. Do not assume you can “bring them later next summer” without checking whether the two-year wait applies.

## D8 and D7 family income floors

**D8 (digital nomad):** main applicant about **€3,680/month** (4× the 2026 minimum wage), **+50%** for a spouse (combined **€5,520**), **+30% (€1,104)** per child/dependent. A family of three (one child) is about **€6,624/month**. **D7 (passive income):** main applicant about **€11,040/year** in savings evidence, **+€5,520** for a spouse, **+€3,312** per child—about **€19,872/year** on account for that same family of three. D7 full cycle often **6–9 months**; post-entry **AIMA** appointments average about **120 days** wait in Lisbon/Porto (sometimes ~**15 days** in quieter regions).

## Schools, western belt, and family budgets

International schools commonly run **€8,000–15,000/year** at primary and **€16,000–26,500/year** for IB Diploma at top schools; bilingual PT/EN options often **€7,500–14,000**. Add enrollment fees **€800–3,500** once, registration **€150–400**, and school bus **€2,200–3,800/year**. A realistic first-year total for two kids in a mid-tier UK/IB school (including one-offs) is **€42,000–62,000**. Most campuses sit in the western belt—**Cascais, Estoril, Sintra, Oeiras**—so central Lisbon families often face **25–50 minute** commutes and need the bus. September seats: apply **October–December** of the prior year; after January choice collapses. Without international school, a family of four often budgets **€2,200–2,500/month**. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**What did Law 61/2025 change for families?**

Adult dependents who do not travel with the main applicant may need to wait **two years** of the principal’s legal stay before joining. File the family **together** when possible.

**How much income does a family of three need on D8?**

About **€6,624/month** after spouse (+50%) and one child (+30%) add-ons on a **€3,680** base.

**What about D7 savings for the same family?**

Roughly **€19,872/year** on account (**€11,040** + **€5,520** + **€3,312**).

**When should we apply to international schools?**

Priority window for September entry is **October–December** of the previous year. Most campuses are in Cascais/Estoril/Sintra/Oeiras.

**What does the first school year really cost?**

For two kids in a mid-tier international school, **€42,000–62,000** including one-off fees and transport is a realistic first-year range.
