## February 2026 DNV thresholds and the NLV trap

Family add-ons: about **+€1,069/month** for the first adult dependent (75% SMI) and **+€357** per child (25% SMI)—a family of three near **€4,275/month**. DNV also needs at least **three months** with the same employer/client and a company that has existed **one year**. Spanish-client work is capped at **20%** of professional income. Spain’s property **Golden Visa** remains closed to new applicants since **April 2025**. If you hold NLV and want DNV, plan a consular re-application—not a quiet UGE switch inside Spain.

## Malaga as Spain’s tech coast

Locals call it Spain’s Silicon Valley strip: Google’s cyber center, Vodafone R&D, and the growing **Málaga TechPark** startup stack. That demand helped push rents **30–40%** since 2022.

## Rents, budgets, and internet

Center one-beds (Soho, Centro Histórico, Malagueta): **€850–1,300/month**. Outside center (Teatinos, Pedregalejo, Huelin): **€650–1,000**. Comfortable solo nomad budgets often **€2,000–2,800/month**. Average internet sits near **214 Mbps** down / **185 Mbps** up, with fiber to **1 Gbps** available.

**What is the Málaga/Spain DNV income floor in 2026?**

**Can I convert Non-Lucrative to Digital Nomad inside Spain?**

Not under 2026 UGE practice—refile through a **consulate abroad**.
