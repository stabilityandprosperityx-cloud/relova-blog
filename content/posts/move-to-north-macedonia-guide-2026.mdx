## Entry, temporary residence, and the nomad option

U.S., EU, UK, and many other passports get **90 days in any 180** visa-free. Temporary residence (**dozvola za privremeno prestojuvanje**) usually runs through a job or your own company, with municipality-dependent income evidence often **€400–600/month**, plus health insurance and a local address. Without local employment, registering a business is the practical long-stay tool. The digital nomad track is the remote-work alternative—verify current income docs before you quit a lease elsewhere. ## Tax, PR, and citizenship clocks

Personal and corporate income tax is a flat **10%**—among Europe’s lowest headline rates. Permanent residence commonly follows **five years** of continuous temporary residence (some sources cite **three** in specific cases—confirm your category). Citizenship typically needs **eight years** of legal stay plus a **Macedonian language** test. The country has been an **EU candidate since 2005**; there is still **no fixed accession date**.

## Skopje costs vs Western Europe

Center one-bedroom rents span roughly **€200–500/month** by source and neighborhood, clustering near **€330–420**. Comfortable solo living **$800–1,200/month** including rent; couples often **$1,200–1,800**. Overall prices sit about **45–60% below** typical Western European baskets—see also [cheapest countries in Europe](https://blog.relova.ai/blog/cheapest-countries-europe-2026).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is there a North Macedonia retiree or passive-income visa?**

No dedicated one. Long stays without a local job usually mean a **company** registration path (or employment), plus the separate **digital nomad** option for remote workers.

**How much income do municipalities want?**

Temporary residence files often show **€400–600/month**, varying by municipality, plus insurance and a local address.

**What are the tax and long-term status clocks?**

Flat **10%** income tax. PR after roughly **five years**; citizenship after about **eight years** with a language test.

**What does Skopje cost in 2026?**

Center one-beds commonly **€330–420/month**. Comfortable solo budgets **$800–1,200**; couples **$1,200–1,800**.

**Is North Macedonia in the EU?**

Not yet—an **EU candidate since 2005** without a locked accession timetable. Plan residency on current Macedonian law, not future EU free movement.
