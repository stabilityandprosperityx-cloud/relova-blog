## January 2026 fee hike and Temporary Resident math

Temporary Resident income floors (UMA-linked from mid-2025) sit near **~$4,400/month** for the last six months **or** about **$72,000–75,000** in savings over the last year. Cards are issued for **one year**, renewable up to **four years** total, then PR. FMM tourist stays can run up to **180 days**, but the officer sets the stamp—do not assume the maximum.

## Cost of living: local vs expat pricing

Oaxaca remains one of Mexico’s more affordable hubs. Comfortable solo budgets often **$1,600–2,400/month**; locals shopping markets can live nearer **$800–1,400**. Furnished 1–2 beds in popular areas: **8,000–15,000 pesos/month (~$400–750)**. Spanish skills and local markets cut the Airbnb-inflated “expat tier” sharply.

## Safety, internet, and peak seasons

State homicide rates near **~8 per 100,000** versus Mexico’s national ~**26**—meaningfully safer than the country average, still with normal urban caution. Fiber in **Centro** and **Jalatlaco** handles video calls. Book housing months ahead for **Guelaguetza (July)** and **Día de Muertos (late October)** price spikes.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does Mexico have a digital nomad visa?**

No. Long stays use **Temporary Resident**; short stays use **FMM** (up to 180 days, officer-dependent).

**What changed in January 2026?**

Visa fees roughly **doubled**—about **MXN $11,140** for a one-year temporary resident and **MXN $13,579** for permanent resident.

**How much income do I need for Temporary Resident?**

About **$4,400/month** for six months of evidence, or roughly **$72–75k** in savings over the prior year.

**Is Oaxaca expensive for a solo remote worker?**

Comfortable budgets often **$1,600–2,400**; local-market living can drop toward **$800–1,400**. Rents in popular areas ~**$400–750**.

**When is housing hardest to find?**

Around **Guelaguetza (July)** and **Día de Muertos (late October)**—book months ahead.
