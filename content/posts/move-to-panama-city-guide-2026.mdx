## Friendly Nations, Pensionado, and Qualified Investor

About **50** “friendly” nationalities qualify (U.S., Canada, UK, most of the EU, Japan, Singapore, Australia, and others). Older chatter about a **$5,000** deposit shortcut is unreliable after **Executive Decree No. 226 (2021)**—confirm the live investment/job path before you wire money. **Pensionado** needs a lifetime pension of **$1,000/month** (+**$250** per dependent), unlocks Law 6 discounts (often **15–50%** on health, transport, entertainment), runs for life, and leads straight to PR. **Qualified Investor** from **$300,000** targets roughly **30-day** processing to PR but **without** a work permit.

## Taxes, dollars, and processing reality

Territorial taxation is the headline advantage for remote earners paid abroad—see [digital nomad taxes](https://blog.relova.ai/blog/digital-nomad-taxes-guide-2026). The country uses the **U.S. dollar**, so USD earners avoid local FX risk. Official timelines are optimistic; practical residence processing often takes **3–6 months**. Citizenship typically follows **five years** of continuous PR plus a basic Spanish interview.

## City budgets, schools, and internet

A comfortable couple budget in Panama City is often **$2,500–3,500/month**; inland towns like Boquete closer to **$1,500–2,500**. Average national internet speeds were about **185 Mbps** in early 2026. International schools run **$10,000–20,000/year**; bilingual private options **$3,000–8,000**.
