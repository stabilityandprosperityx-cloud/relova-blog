## Cost of living vs Lisbon

Expect Porto to undercut Lisbon by roughly **25–35%** across housing and daily spend. Solo non-rent living often **€800–1,000/month**; with rent, a comfortable solo total is about **€1,550–1,650** (~**$1,700–1,800**). A family of four’s non-rent basket is often cited near **€2,026/month**. Average internet sits around **100 Mbps**. ## Neighborhoods, transport, and buying

Expat favorites include **Cedofeita, Bonfim, Boavista**, and coastal **Foz do Douro**. A day transit pass is about **€7.35**. Buying a two-bedroom near the center often starts around **$250,000**—still cheaper than comparable Lisbon stock, but no longer “giveaway” pricing. ## Visa fit: why D7 works better here

Portugal’s **D7** passive-income threshold (~**€820–870/month**) aligns more realistically with Porto rents than with Lisbon’s center market. That does not remove document or insurance requirements—see [Portugal D7 guide](https://blog.relova.ai/blog/portugal-d7-visa-guide)—but it does mean the legal floor and livable budget can overlap in Bonfim/Cedofeita in a way Lisbon rarely allows.
