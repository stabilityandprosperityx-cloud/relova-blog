## Family visa math in 2026

**Digital Nomad:** remote work for a foreign employer allowed; family-of-three income near **€4,070/month**. **Non-Lucrative Visa:** **€28,800/year** for the main applicant plus **€7,200/year** per dependent—and **no work at all**, including remote. Do not plan on a new property Golden Visa; that door closed for new filings on **3 April 2025**.

## Budgets, schools, and city choice

A family of four on **public school** often spends **€2,000–4,500/month** by city—Barcelona/Madrid at the top, Valencia/Málaga/Seville cheaper. International school adds roughly **€500–1,500/month per child** (other estimates **€5,000–15,000/year**). Public schools accept resident children regardless of Spanish level; younger kids adapt faster, while teens (**14+**) often stay in international school for university continuity—see [international schools](https://blog.relova.ai/blog/best-international-schools-expats-2026).

## Arrival admin: empadronamiento, TIE, and August

First stop after landing: **empadronamiento** at the town hall—without it you cannot enroll kids or register for public healthcare. Non-EU national-visa holders must get a **TIE** within **one month** of entry. Avoid the **August trap**: much of Spain’s admin slows or stops for holiday—do not schedule critical town-hall visits then.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How much income does a family of three need on the nomad visa?**

About **€4,070/month** pre-tax after the February 2026 minimum-wage update (**€2,849** + **€916** + **€305**).

**Can we still apply for Spain’s Golden Visa?**

Not as a new applicant—the route closed on **3 April 2025**.

**What is empadronamiento and why does it matter?**

Town-hall address registration. Without it you cannot school children or enroll in public healthcare. Get your **TIE** within **one month** if you entered on a national visa.

**How long does a family move really take?**

Plan **6–12 months** from decision to settled life—and avoid critical admin in **August**.

**Public or international school?**

Public schools take resident kids without Spanish. Teens (**14+**) often stay international for university continuity; budget **€500–1,500/month** per child if you go private international.
