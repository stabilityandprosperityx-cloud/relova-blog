## Visa routes that actually fit Tenerife

NLV still needs about **€28,800/year** for a solo applicant and bans remote work; the digital nomad route is for people who keep foreign employers/clients. Full NLV numbers and the work ban are covered in [Spain non-lucrative visa](https://blog.relova.ai/blog/spain-non-lucrative-visa-guide-2026) and [Spain digital nomad visa](https://blog.relova.ai/blog/spain-digital-nomad-visa). After entry you still need NIE/TIE and island admin—budget weeks, not a weekend.

## Rent, budgets, and the IGIC advantage

Studios in **Santa Cruz** often start near **€500/month**; tourist belts like **Costa Adeje** more often from **€800/month**. A comfortable solo budget is **€2,200–3,000/month** net including rent, a car, and occasional restaurants. **€1,500–2,000** is workable if you skip the car and eat out rarely. Local average pay on the Canaries is only about **€1,200/month**—useful context when your expat spend looks “high” to neighbors. A family of four’s non-housing basket is often cited around **€2,886/month** on Tenerife (**€3,008** on Gran Canaria). **IGIC at 7%** (vs 21% mainland IVA) softens goods and services costs island-wide.

## Housing market heat and schools

Canary property prices rose about **15.4% year-on-year in 2025**—among Spain’s fastest—and rents climbed about **12.8%** the same year. Do not assume 2023 lease quotes still hold. International schools commonly run **€3,500/year** at primary up to about **€8,000/year** higher up. For mainland city comparisons, see [Valencia](https://blog.relova.ai/blog/move-to-valencia-spain-guide-2026) and [Málaga](https://blog.relova.ai/blog/move-to-malaga-guide-2026).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Which visa do most expats use for Tenerife?**

Spain’s **Non-Lucrative Visa** (~**€28,800/year**, no work) or the **Digital Nomad Visa** if you keep foreign remote income. Tenerife does not have a separate island visa.

**How much should a single person budget?**

Comfortable living is often **€2,200–3,000/month**. Lean living can work at **€1,500–2,000** without a car and with few restaurants.

**Why is Tenerife cheaper on taxes at the till?**

The Canaries use **IGIC at 7%** instead of mainland **21% IVA** on many goods and services.

**Is housing still cheap?**

Relative to Madrid/Barcelona in places, yes—but 2025 saw roughly **+15.4%** property prices and **+12.8%** rents. Santa Cruz studios from ~**€500**; Adeje more from ~**€800**.

**What do international schools cost?**

Often **€3,500–8,000/year** depending on level. Model that on top of the ~**€2,886** family non-housing basket.
