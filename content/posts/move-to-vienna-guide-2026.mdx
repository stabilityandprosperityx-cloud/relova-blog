**move to Vienna 2026** for non-EU talent usually means the points-based **Red-White-Red Card (RWR)**: **70/100** points for highly qualified applicants or **55/90** for shortage/key workers. Other key workers need at least **€3,465 gross/month** in 2026. The **EU Blue Card** floor is **€55,678/year gross** (including 13th/14th salary). ## RWR, Blue Card, and the Plus upgrade

After **21 months** of qualifying employment you can apply for **RWR Card Plus**—it drops the employer lock, opens the labour market, and runs for **three years**. Permanent residence (**Daueraufenthalt-EU**) typically follows **five years** of legal stay (RWR + Plus). Citizenship is often **10 years**, or **6** with exceptional integration. Compare Germanic hubs with [Move to Germany](https://blog.relova.ai/blog/move-to-germany-guide-2026).

## Language, processing, and fees

German is not mandatory to file, but about **73%** of jobs want at least **B2**. **A2** is needed for RWR Plus; citizenship usually wants **B1**. Official processing is up to **eight weeks**; practice often runs **6–12**. RWR Card Plus fee is **€218** from **1 January 2026** (was €212).

## Vienna rents, transit, and monthly budgets

Center one-beds: **€800–1,400/month** (average near **€1,100**); outer districts **€700–1,200**. Comfortable solo living spans **€2,200–3,600/month** by lifestyle. The **Jahreskarte** annual transit pass is **€467/year**—the famous **€365** fare ended after 2025. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**What salary do I need for Vienna’s RWR track in 2026?**

Other key workers need about **€3,465 gross/month**. Blue Card sits at **€55,678/year gross**. Points targets are **70/100** or **55/90** depending on category.

**When can I switch employers?**

After about **21 months**, **RWR Card Plus** removes the employer tie for a three-year card.

**How long to PR and a passport?**

**Is German required on day one?**
