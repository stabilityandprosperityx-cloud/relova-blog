## U.S. Social Security abroad (and the Fairness Act)

Most retirees can receive Social Security overseas—confirm your country on SSA’s **Payments Abroad Screening Tool**. **International Direct Deposit** works where the U.S. has banking arrangements. Every **1–2 years** SSA may send a questionnaire; ignore it and payments pause. The **Social Security Fairness Act** (signed **5 January 2025**) repealed **WEP** and **GPO** for benefits from **January 2024**; many people saw back-pay adjustments around **April 2025**. Do **not** confuse retirement benefits with **SSI**—Supplemental Security Income usually stops after a full calendar month or **30 consecutive days** outside the U.S. Tax angle: [U.S. expat taxes](https://blog.relova.ai/blog/us-expat-taxes-guide-2026).

## UK State Pension: the frozen-pension trap

Uprating continues if you live in the **UK, EEA, Switzerland**, or a bilateral-agreement country that includes indexation (e.g. **USA**). In Australia, Canada, New Zealand, South Africa and many other places the pension is **frozen** at the first award amount despite inflation—affecting about **500,000** British pensioners worldwide. Full new State Pension for 2026/27 is about **£230.25–241.30/week** for those who still receive uprating. You need **35 years** of NI for a full pension and at least **10 years** for any payment. Abroad, voluntary **Class 3** NI can still build years; reduced-rate **Class 2** voluntary contributions end in **April 2026**. Annual **life certificates** matter—miss one and payments stall. ## Paperwork that stops payments

Both systems fail administratively more often than legally: unanswered SSA questionnaires, missed UK life certificates, and wrong assumptions about SSI vs Social Security. Build a calendar reminder before you book long travel.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I get U.S. Social Security while living abroad?**

Usually yes in most countries—verify with SSA’s **Payments Abroad Screening Tool** and keep answering periodic questionnaires.

**What did the Social Security Fairness Act change?**

It repealed **WEP** and **GPO** for benefits from **January 2024** (law signed **5 January 2025**). Many recipients received back-pay around **April 2025**.

**Why is the UK State Pension “frozen” for some expats?**

Outside the UK/EEA/Switzerland and certain treaty countries, the award stops uprating. Roughly **500,000** British pensioners—especially in Australia, Canada, NZ, and South Africa—are affected.

**How many NI years do I need?**

**35** for a full new State Pension; at least **10** for any payment. Class 3 voluntary NI can still fill gaps; Class 2 voluntary relief ends **April 2026**.

**Is SSI the same as Social Security?**

No. **SSI** typically stops after **30 days** (or a full calendar month) outside the U.S.—do not assume retirement Social Security rules apply.
