## Step-by-step process in 2026

1. **Consulate / VFS filing** in your country of residence: passport, application form, **3 months** of income evidence at **€3,680/month**, solo savings of about **€11,040** (12× minimum wage), apostilled/translated criminal record, health insurance, and Portuguese housing proof.  
2. **Pay consular fees** of about **€90–120**.  
3. **Wait 60–90 working days** for processing.  
4. **Receive the visa stamp** valid **120 days**—your entry window.  
5. **Enter Portugal** and prepare the AIMA file.  
6. **Book AIMA ASAP**—backlog often means **2–4 months** wait (better than 6+ months in 2024). Bring biometrics and updated bank statements.  
7. **Pay the residence-card fee** of about **€170** (after the March 2026 fee review).  
8. **Receive the card by post** in **2–12 weeks**.  
9. **Status:** initial residence **2 years**, then renewal for **3 years**, then PR/citizenship after **5 years** of continuous residence (typically **183+ days/year** in Portugal, no more than **6 consecutive months** abroad).

## Common refusal and delay triggers

Leases under **12 months** (AIMA often rejects under **6**). Missing **NISS**. Income claims that do not match bank statements. Travel insurance instead of residency-grade cover. Missing **Modelo 2** lease registration in Finanças—from **August 2025** tenants can register themselves if the landlord does not. Plane tickets alone as proof of lawful entry are frequently insufficient.

Government fees alone for one applicant total about **€260–400** (visa + card), excluding translations, apostilles, insurance, and rent.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the D8 income floor in 2026?**

**€3,680/month** (4× the **€920** minimum wage), plus about **€11,040** savings for a solo applicant.

**I filed in 2025 on €3,480—am I safe?**

Not if your **AIMA appointment** falls in 2026. Finances are judged on the appointment date—prepare for **€3,680**.

**How long does the whole process take?**

Consular processing **60–90 working days**, then often **2–4 months** to AIMA, then **2–12 weeks** for the card.

**What lease length does AIMA want?**

Prefer **12 months**. Under **6 months** is a common refusal trigger. Register the lease (**Modelo 2**) in Finanças.

**How much are government fees alone?**

About **€260–400** for one person (visa + residence card), before apostilles, insurance, and housing.
