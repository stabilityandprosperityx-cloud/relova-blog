Moving with children compresses every timeline. Start **6–12 months** out — international school seats in hot cities are the constraint that most often breaks a “we’ll figure it out after we land” plan.

## Visa for the whole household

Dependent rules are country-specific. Some states want **school enrolment proof before** issuing child visas; others reverse the order. Verify the sequence for *your* destination instead of copying a generic checklist.

Income floors usually scale: main applicant + about **50%** of the base for a spouse + **25–30%** per child. Portugal’s **D7** pattern is a clear example — roughly **€920/month** for the main applicant, **+50%** for a spouse, **+30%** per dependent. Build the spreadsheet before you fall in love with a neighbourhood.

Children’s paperwork — birth certificates, vaccination cards, custody documents when relevant — almost always needs sworn translation and often apostille. Start legalisation early; it is slow and non-negotiable at biometrics.

## School: when and how to search

Begin outreach inside the 6–12 month window. Under **5–6**, children usually adapt fastest; strong language acquisition commonly holds through about age **8**. Teen moves hit social life harder — for middle/high school, international programmes (**IB**, British, American) can reduce academic whiplash even when they cost more.

**Spain:** international fees run about **€6,000–28,000/year** by programme and city, but **public schooling is free from ages 3–18**; families mainly pay meals, aftercare, and trips. In **2026**, Euronews named Spain a top family-relocation country — public access is a big reason.

**Malaysia:** fees span roughly **RM12,000–145,000/year** across **250+** schools; from **2026**, budget the **6%** service tax on amounts above **RM60,000**.

## Healthcare for children

Before you move, confirm whether the local public system covers routine paediatrics and the vaccination schedule you already started — and whether you still need private top-up for faster specialist access. Do not assume your adult IPMI automatically mirrors paediatric networks.

## What it costs in practice

Initial relocation cash for a family of four often lands between **€5,000–30,000** depending on flights, visas, deposits, shipping, and the first-month buffer. Ongoing burn varies sharply by city:

| Destination | Family-of-4 comfort signal | School note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lisbon area | **€6,000–6,500/month** including school costs for many households | Cheaper outside Lisbon |
| Spain (mixed public/private) | Lower if public seats work; rises fast with private international | Public 3–18 is the cost lever |
| Malaysia | Fees dominate; add **6%** tax above RM60k from 2026 | Wide fee band |

Family relocation succeeds when visas, school letters, and paediatric continuity are scheduled as three parallel projects — not as afterthoughts to the parents’ job offer. Sequence those three, and the rest of the move becomes logistics instead of crisis management.


Prep children with age-appropriate honesty: show photos of the school and neighbourhood, keep one stable routine (meals, bedtime) through the move, and avoid selling the destination as permanent paradise — unmet expectations hurt teens especially. Budget emotional recovery time in the first term; academic catch-up and friendship rebuilding are real workloads.

For dual-career households, sequence whose job unlocks the visa and whose job can pause. The dependent spouse’s work rights vary widely; confirm them before assuming both careers restart on month one. If one parent will be the primary school liaison, factor language classes for that parent into the 6–12 month plan alongside the children’s enrolment.


Keep a family “go bag” of originals and copies of every child’s document in both carry-ons, not the checked container. If a school or clinic appointment lands before the shipment arrives, you still need vaccination proof and birth certificates on day three. Redundancy is boring and decisive.


Document decisions as you go: which visa category you chose and why, which temporary address you booked, which school waitlists you joined. Future-you will need that trail when a consulate, landlord, or insurer asks for a timeline. Relocation amnesia is real after the adrenaline fades — write the rationale while it is fresh, in the same folder as your scans.


If grandparents or other caregivers will visit for long stretches, check visitor-visa rules and school holiday calendars early — unpaid childcare help is a budget line that still has immigration constraints. Align visit windows with your temporary-housing end date so you are not hosting family in a studio Airbnb during the hardest adjustment weeks.
