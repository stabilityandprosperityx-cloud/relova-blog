Tbilisi on **$600 a month** is a 2021–2022 story. Central rents have roughly **doubled in dollars since 2022**. You can still live here on a modest remote salary; you cannot paste old blog totals into a 2026 lease. Across sources, **modest** all-in often lands **$720–1,070** (rent + food + transport). **Comfortable** singles show **$1,350–2,500**, with central-heavy versions at **$1,500–2,500** and couples **$2,500–4,000**.

Below are three **line-item** scenarios, then how **seasonality** can still save you 10–25% on rent, then everyday prices so you can catch fake “cheap Caucasus” lists.

## Three monthly scenarios (line by line)

**A. Studio in Saburtalo — lean**

| Line | USD |
| --- | ---: |
| Rent | 400 |
| Utilities + internet | 80 |
| Groceries | 200 |
| Restaurants (6–8×) | 100 |
| Coffee | 50 |
| Transport | 30 |
| Phone | 10 |
| Misc | 80 |
| **Total** | **~950** |

This is the “I cook, I metro, I do not live in Vera Instagram” version. It sits inside the modest **$720–1,070** band if you shave restaurants, and at the top of it if you do not.

**B. One-bed in Vake or Vera — comfortable**

| Line | USD |
| --- | ---: |
| Rent | 650 |
| Utilities | 100 |
| Groceries | 250 |
| Restaurants (12–15×) | 200 |
| Coffee / drinks | 100 |
| Gym | 50 |
| Transport (more Bolt) | 80 |
| Insurance | 28 |
| Misc | 150 |
| **Total** | **~1,600** |

This matches the comfortable **$1,350–2,500** middle. A renovated Vera one-bed at **$700–1,200** (see the neighbourhood guide) pushes you toward the top of that band before you eat.

**C. Couple or small family — central-ish**

Use **$2,500–4,000** as the planning envelope: a larger flat, two people eating out, school or daycare if relevant, and less tolerance for winter heating surprises. Two people cannot live the $950 Saburtalo studio script without friction.

If your bookmarks still say “Tbilisi for $600,” add **30–50%** before you talk to a landlord. That correction is the 2026 update.

Couples should not double the $950 column and call it a plan. A second person adds food and transport faster than they share rent; the **$2,500–4,000** envelope exists because two comfortable lives in Vake are not two Saburtalo studios stacked. If one person works from cafés, use the $1,600 café/coffee lines, not the $950 home-espresso lines, or the “we only spend a thousand” myth returns in week three.

## Seasonality: when landlords blink

**November–February** is low season. Landlords flex; asking rents can drop **10–25%**. **May–September** stacks tourists and new expats; you pay the peak. If your visa and job allow a winter arrival, that is the cheapest honest hack still left after the post-2022 doubling.

Sign a winter lease with a documented price, or at least negotiate from winter comps. Do not use August asking prices as your annual average.

## Daily prices so you can sanity-check blogs

- Khachapuri in an ordinary place: **$3–6**
- Glass of Georgian wine: **$4–7**
- Serious dinner for two with wine: **$30–60**
- Flat white in a Vake/Vera café: **$4.50–7** (London/Berlin territory)
- Corner-shop espresso: **$2**
- Wolt / Bolt Food with delivery: **$8–15** a drop

Those café prices are why the $950 script assumes you are not doing two flat whites a day in Vera. The $1,600 script already contains that habit.

Two more lines people forget until month two: **deposits** (often one to two months, in dollars or lari — get the currency in writing) and **one-off setup** (SIM, used heater, extra blankets, a desk that is not the kitchen table). Those do not belong in the $950 monthly total, but they belong in the money you land with. A $950 life with a $400 deposit and a $200 setup week is a $1,550 first month.

If you work in a café, the $4.50–7 flat white is a coworking fee. Count it that way. If you work at home in Saburtalo, the $2 espresso is the realistic daily. Mix the two without updating the spreadsheet and you will swear Tbilisi “got expensive overnight” when you simply changed neighbourhoods.

Budget Tbilisi in 2026 as **~$950 lean Saburtalo**, **~$1,600 comfortable Vake/Vera**, **$2,500–4,000** for two in the centre — then time your lease for winter if you can. The doubling since 2022 is the fact that kills old $600 posts; seasonality is the fact that still gives you a lever.
