## Visa-free stays, border runs, and the March 2026 work-permit law

U.S., UK, EU, Canadian, and Australian passports commonly get **365 days** visa-free. After a year, a short **border run** to Armenia, Turkey, or Azerbaijan can reset the clock—but repeated resets draw immigration questions. From **1 March 2026**, work permits apply when you work **for a Georgian employer/registration**; foreign-only remote income is generally treated differently—confirm current guidance if your setup is hybrid. ## Tax residency, 1% small business, and Virtual Zone

Tax residency usually starts after **183 days** in a 12-month period—visa-free presence alone does **not** auto-make you a tax resident. To pay into Georgia’s system, many freelancers register as an **Individual Entrepreneur** with **Small Business Status**: **1% turnover tax** up to **500,000 GEL/year** (~**$165,000–190,000** depending on FX), then **3%** above that. IT exporters may use **Virtual Zone** for **0%** corporate tax on exported services. Broader framing: [digital nomad taxes](https://blog.relova.ai/blog/digital-nomad-taxes-guide-2026).

## Budgets, Vake rent, banking, and PR

Comfortable solo living is often **$1,200–2,000/month** including rent, food, and coworking. A one-bed in **Vake** commonly **$500–800**. Coworking internet is usually a steady **100+ Mbps**; home Wi‑Fi can swing **20–100 Mbps** with dropouts. **TBC Bank** or **Bank of Georgia** accounts open quickly with a passport and address proof (even a hotel booking at the start). Permanent residence is typically available after **six years** of legal stay.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does Georgia have a digital nomad visa?**

Not really as a separate product in 2026—the practical path is **365-day visa-free** stay for many passports. “Remotely from Georgia” is largely outdated.

**What changed on 1 March 2026?**

Work permits became required for foreigners working **in/for Georgian companies**. Pure remote workers paid only from abroad are generally outside that rule—verify if your case is mixed.

**When do I become a Georgian tax resident?**

Usually after **183 days** in 12 months. Visa-free presence alone does not automatically create tax residency.

**What is the 1% tax everyone mentions?**

**Individual Entrepreneur + Small Business Status**: **1%** of turnover up to **500,000 GEL/year**, then **3%**. IT exporters may look at **Virtual Zone** (0% on exported services).

**What does Tbilisi cost, and how long to PR?**

Comfortable solo budgets **$1,200–2,000**; Vake one-beds **$500–800**. PR commonly after **six years** of legal residence.
