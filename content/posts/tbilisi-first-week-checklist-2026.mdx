## Day-by-day first-week sequence

1. **Day 1 — SIM.** Buy from **Magticom, Silknet, or Cellfie** (airport or any shop) with your **passport**—registration is mandatory. Expect about **5 GEL** for the SIM plus a **15–40 GEL/month** data pack.  
2. **Days 1–3 — temporary housing.** Arrive with **1–2 weeks** of Airbnb/short-term already booked. Do not sign a long lease sight-unseen from abroad.  
3. **Days 2–5 — walk neighborhoods.** **Vera** (walkable, cafés) and **Vake** (more family-oriented) remain the main expat picks; also sample Saburtalo.  
4. **Same window — bank account.** Prefer a **central** **TBC Bank** or **Bank of Georgia** branch (district offices more often refuse foreigners without clear reason). Bring passport, local number, ideally **6–12 months** of home-bank statements, and a lease if you have one. AML checks tightened in **2026**—“passport only” is no longer a sure thing. Plan **30–60 minutes** in branch.

## Banking, leases, IE registration, and insurance

After you pick a district, hunt long-term rentals on **SS.ge** and local Facebook groups. If you will operate locally as a freelancer, register **Individual Entrepreneur + Small Business Status** (**1%** turnover tax up to **500,000 GEL/year**) at a **Public Service Hall** with passport and a legal Georgian address. From **1 March 2026**, work-permit rules affect Georgian-company employment; IE registration may need extra checks—confirm before you file. Foreigners **cannot** use Georgia’s public health system—buy private cover from day one (entry guidance often cites at least **GEL 30,000** coverage).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why is the SIM the first errand?**

Banks need a **Georgian number** for SMS codes. Without it, account opening stalls.

**Which bank branch should I use?**

A **central** TBC or Bank of Georgia branch. District offices more often refuse foreigners without clear reason.

**Can I use public healthcare as a foreigner?**

No. Private insurance is required from the start (often framed as **GEL 30,000+** cover for entry).

**What changed for IE registration in 2026?**

From **1 March 2026**, work-permit rules tightened around Georgian employment/IE setups—confirm current Public Service Hall requirements before registering.

**Where do expats look for long leases?**

**SS.ge** and Facebook groups—after you have walked Vera/Vake (and maybe Saburtalo) in person.
