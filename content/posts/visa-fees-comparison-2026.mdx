**visa fees comparison 2026** is less about the stamp fee and more about the **income floor** behind it. More than **65 countries** now offer some form of digital-nomad permission. ## How to read nomad “fees” in 2026

Government stamp costs are often small next to proof-of-funds. Croatia (~**€2,540/month**), Spain (~**€2,850**), Italy (~**€2,700–2,800**), and Portugal D8 (~**€3,280–3,680** depending on source) sit in a similar European band. Dubai cites **$3,500** (employee) / **$5,000** (business) with ~**$334** in fees. Korea’s F-1-D spans roughly **$33,000–66,000/year** by age/region; Japan’s designated activities track sits near **$67,000–70,000/year** for up to **six non-renewable months**. Malaysia DE Rantau is often framed near **$2,000/month**; Mexico and Colombia sit lower but vary by consulate. Broader context: [digital nomad visas](https://blog.relova.ai/blog/digital-nomad-visa-complete-guide-2025) and [Europe nomad comparison](https://blog.relova.ai/blog/nomad-visa-europe-comparison).

## Who actually leads to permanent residence

Among popular picks, **Portugal, Spain, Italy, and Croatia** are the ones most often described as leading toward PR/citizenship after about **five years** (Croatia usually via a separate post-nomad path). UAE, Japan, South Korea, and Thailand LTR are typically **temporary** without a direct PR handoff—UAE needs a separate Golden Visa story. Fast PR options elsewhere: [permanent residency routes](https://blog.relova.ai/blog/permanent-residency-abroad-fastest-routes-2026).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the cheapest “nomad” setup in 2026?**

Georgia’s **365-day visa-free** stay for many passports still has **€0** government fee and no income floor—though it is not a formal nomad visa.

**Which popular programs lead toward PR?**

**Portugal, Spain, Italy, and Croatia** (Croatia often via a follow-on path) are the usual five-year stories. UAE/Japan/Korea/Thailand LTR are mostly temporary.

**Do fees or income floors matter more?**

Income floors. Stamp fees of **€80–$334** are noise next to proving **€2,500–$6,600+/month** for months of bank statements.

**How many countries offer nomad visas now?**

More than **65** in some form as of 2026—always re-check the live consulate checklist before you apply.

**What documents do most programs want?**

Typically **3–6 months** of bank statements plus insurance and remote-work proof—rules tightened in several hubs in early 2026.
